# Instagram Reel / TikTok (about 30 seconds)

## Structure: three blocks
1. **Hook (0–3 s).** Couple of words that stop the scroll. Max 7 spoken words.
2. **Content (3–25 s).** One intro sentence, then exactly three numbered points (1. 2. 3.).
   Each point: a short title and one line of explanation. The 3rd point is the most important.
3. **CTA (25–30 s).** One clear ask in one line. Max 15 words. Only one ask.

## Output
A timed script with SPOKEN lines and ON SCREEN text. No shot or camera descriptions. No sticker.

## Rules
- Target 65–75 spoken words in total.
- On-screen cards: 6 words or fewer. Up to 5 hashtags at the end, if any.
- Baghdadi, with گ و چ used naturally. No greeting. No sign-off.
- Figures are `{{رقمك}}` placeholders. Never invent numbers.

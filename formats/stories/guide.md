# Instagram Story video (30 seconds, 5 posts a day)

## Structure: three blocks
1. **Hook (0–3 s).** Couple of words that stop the scroll. Max 7 spoken words.
2. **Content (3–25 s).** One intro sentence, then exactly three numbered points (١. ٢. ٣.).
   Each point: a short title and one line of explanation. The 3rd point is the most important.
3. **CTA (25–30 s).** One reason to follow or come back, with one interactive sticker. Max 15 words.

## Output
A timed script with SPOKEN lines and ON SCREEN text. No shot or camera descriptions.
Each line may carry several fields separated by " | ".

## Rules
- Target 65–75 spoken words in total.
- On-screen cards: 6 words or fewer.
- Baghdadi, with گ and چ used naturally. No greeting. No sign-off.
- Figures are `{{رقمك}}` placeholders. Never invent numbers.

## Five daily angles (rotate, never repeat the same angle twice in a row)
1. Myth-busting: a belief, then the truth.
2. Quick tip: one thing to do today.
3. Behind the scenes: honest, unpolished.
4. Customer pain: told without names.
5. Offer or ask: a reason to act now.
